# GymRats 💪

**GymRats** is a full-featured web application designed to manage a fitness club. It allows administrators and users to handle memberships, diets, workout plans, online courses, and training sessions.

## Features

### 🧾 Memberships Panel
- Add new membership types.
- Edit the price of existing memberships.

### 🍽️ Diets & Workout Plans Panels
- Upload sample meal plans and workout plans in PDF format.
- Delete existing diet or workout plan files.

### 🎓 Courses Panel
- Add a new course via the **"Buy Course"** button.

### 🧮 BMI Calculator
- Enter your weight and height.
- Instantly get feedback about your BMI status (underweight, normal, overweight).

### 🏋️‍♂️ Personal and Group Training
- Schedule personal training sessions with trainers.
- Sign up for group classes and manage your bookings.
